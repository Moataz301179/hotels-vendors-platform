# HotelsVendors Business Engine

## Core proposition

**Your Virtual Shadow for smarter procurement.**

HotelsVendors is a four-sided hospitality procurement network: **Hotels · Suppliers · Carriers · Funders**.

The intelligence loop is:

**WATCH → FIND → ACT → OUTCOME**

The Virtual Shadow may identify money leaks, procurement inefficiencies, supplier demand/opportunities, logistics opportunities and funding signals only from authorized evidence. A signal without supporting evidence is not a business fact.

## Economic chain

`Evidence → Finding → Opportunity → Authorized Action → Commercial Outcome → Revenue Event`

### Hotel value
- Procurement visibility and spend intelligence.
- Supplier discovery and RFQ workflows.
- Quote and order decisions.
- Savings opportunities and realized outcomes.
- Qualified funding signals.

### Supplier value
- Qualified hotel demand.
- RFQs and quote opportunities.
- Orders, fulfillment and repeat commercial activity.

### Carrier value
- Delivery/fulfillment opportunities.
- Assignment and completion status.

### Funder value
- Qualified external funding referrals.
- Referral/conversion status.

HotelsVendors is not a lender and does not approve or decline financing. External funders make financing decisions.

## Revenue events

Revenue must be tied to a real underlying business event. Candidate loops:

1. Procurement transaction: RFQ → quote → order → completed transaction → applicable platform fee.
2. Supplier acquisition: qualified demand → supplier conversion → commercial value.
3. Funding referral: qualified signal → external referral → external conversion → documented commission.

If the underlying event cannot be traced, the revenue event is not counted.
