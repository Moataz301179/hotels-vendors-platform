## Selected world
A contemporary hospitality trade ledger whose precise evidence/document grammar becomes a living commercial signal path. Warm mineral paper, charcoal ink, restrained ice-blue/cobalt signal, hairline rules, tabular data alignment, deliberate negative space, and one animated trace connecting source activity → connected context → potential finding → human action. It is a digital evidence instrument, not a faux paper prop or generic AI illustration.

## Challenger weighing — exactly two axes
Axes: audience identification (does a hospitality participant recognize the business world?) and product clarity (does the form make HV's actual signal-to-action mechanism easier to understand?).

1. **Metal forging / power hammer — declined.** Audience identification: low; product clarity: low. Borrowed discipline: discrete, inspectable state change per step. Applied as selectable signal stages with an explicit evidence panel, not forge imagery, heat, or particles.
2. **Theater cyclorama / dawn lighting — declined.** Audience identification: low; product clarity: low. Borrowed discipline: the state of a process should be legible as it changes. Applied as one restrained moving signal trace plus a static reduced-motion fallback, not theatrical gradients or stage imagery.
3. **Variable-font specimen — competitive.** Audience identification: moderate; product clarity: high. Kept discipline: strong contrast between display typography and compact, tabular data labels. Declined the morphing-glyph interface because the product needs to explain business evidence, not typography.
4. **VU-meter bridge — competitive.** Audience identification: moderate; product clarity: high. Kept discipline: status should read at a glance across repeated channels. Applied as aligned source/context/finding/action stages with explicit active/passed states, not meter faces or audio-reactive behavior.
5. **Acetate-tab reference manual — competitive.** Audience identification: moderate; product clarity: high. Kept discipline: layered information hierarchy and clear section progression. Applied as a structured folio with source references and a consistent stage rail, not transparent acetate, physical hinges, or imitation materials.
6. **Teen-magazine quiz spread — declined.** Audience identification: low; product clarity: low. Borrowed discipline: the user's current choice must be unmistakable. Applied as a keyboard-focusable selected signal stage with an updated evidence explanation, not collage, torn edges, or quiz scoring.

## Final choice
The Signal Folio remains the sole visual world. Challengers donate interaction discipline only; none of their visual costumes are mixed into the page. The world must extend coherently to platform, marketplace, authentication, role workspaces and admin while preserving the four-actor model, tenant boundaries and funding boundary.
